## The Challenge

Red Bull's brand is deeply associated with extreme sports and adrenaline-driven experiences. While this positioning has built strong brand equity, it limits relevance in everyday consumption contexts — particularly among young professionals seeking sustained energy and productivity rather than a single adrenaline spike.

## My Role & Approach

I developed this independently as a positioning and campaign exploration, focused on expanding usage occasions into workplace environments through product, placement, and promotion — without abandoning what already makes the brand distinct.

## Research

A few market trends shaped the direction of this concept:

- Increasing demand for functional, low-sugar energy solutions.
- Gen Z and Millennials prioritizing productivity alongside work-life balance.
- Growth in workplace wellness and "performance optimization" culture.

The key insight: consumers increasingly don't separate "work mode" and "life mode" — they want brands that support both.

## Target & Strategic Shift

**Target persona**: young professionals (23–40), career-driven but health-conscious, seeking energy without compromising identity.

**Strategic shift**: from adrenaline and risk, to ambition and performance across contexts. The reframed idea: Red Bull isn't just for peak moments — it fuels everyday ambition.

## The Concept: "You Can Be Both"

**Product**
Workplace-oriented packaging (concept name: "Morning Momentum") and desk kits paired with productivity tools.

**Placement**
Coworking spaces, office vending machines, and corporate wellness programs — meeting young professionals in environments already associated with ambition.

**Promotion**
LinkedIn-first campaigns, an "After Work" event series, and partnerships with creators who embody both career success and personal passion.

## Reflection

This project showed how strong brands can evolve without losing identity. The goal isn't to replace the core audience, but to layer new meaning on top of existing associations. The most effective repositioning strategies expand relevance while preserving emotional consistency.
